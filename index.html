Controls

Play with the mouse alone, the keyboard alone, or mix the two. Moving the mouse takes the wheel; pressing a movement key hands it straight back.

Mouse easiest

  • Move mouse — your ship flies to the cursor
  • Left click — fire (hold to keep firing)
  • Right hold — boost (drains gas)
  • Middle click — dodge blink (45 gas, brief immunity)
  • Wheel — barrage

Keyboard

  • WASD / Arrows — move
  • Shift — boost
  • Space — dodge blink
  • Enter / J — fire (hold to keep firing)
  • B — barrage
  • Esc pause · M mute · F fullscreen · H controls

Touch

  • Left stick — move
  • Fire / Dodge / Barrage — tap
  • Boost — hold

Gamepad

  • Left stick / D-pad — move
  • A dodge · X fire · Y barrage · RT boost

The goal

  • Shoot the red ship down before it shoots you down.
  • Each win pays credits; spend them on upgrades.

The campaign is a 15-level run: each level's bot is faster, smarter, and better armed, rewards grow with the depth, and your HP carries between levels (with a small patch-up) — die and the run is over. Grab the supply drops drifting off the center line: repairs, gas, and ammo surges — smarter bots race you for them. Finish a level flawless, fast, or accurate for bonus credits. Legacy modes keep the original four difficulties, where chaining wins multiplies your rewards.
