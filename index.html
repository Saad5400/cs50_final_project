HOW TO PLAY

⌨️ Keyboard / 🖱 Mouse

  • WASD / Arrows — move
  • Shift — boost (drains gas)
  • Space — dodge blink (45 gas, brief immunity)
  • Enter / J / Click — fire
  • B / Right-click — barrage
  • Esc pause · M mute · F fullscreen

📱 Touch

  • Left stick — move
  • FIRE / DODGE / BARRAGE — tap
  • BOOST — hold

🎮 Gamepad

  • Left stick / D-pad — move
  • A dodge · X fire · Y barrage · RT boost
