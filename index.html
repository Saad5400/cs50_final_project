How to play

Keyboard & mouse

  • WASD / Arrows — move
  • Shift — boost (drains gas)
  • Space — dodge blink (45 gas, brief immunity)
  • Enter / J / Click — fire
  • B / Right-click — barrage
  • Esc pause · M mute · F fullscreen

Touch

  • Left stick — move
  • Fire / Dodge / Barrage — tap
  • Boost — hold

Gamepad

  • Left stick / D-pad — move
  • A dodge · X fire · Y barrage · RT boost

The campaign is a 15-level run: each level's bot is faster, smarter, and better armed, rewards grow with the depth, and your HP carries between levels (with a small patch-up) — die and the run is over. Grab the supply drops drifting off the center line: repairs, gas, and ammo surges — smarter bots race you for them. Finish a level flawless, fast, or accurate for bonus credits. Legacy modes keep the original four difficulties, where chaining wins multiplies your rewards.
